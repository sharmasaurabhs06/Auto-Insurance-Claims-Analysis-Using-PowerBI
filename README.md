# 🚗 Auto Insurance Claims Analysis Dashboard

This project presents an interactive **Power BI dashboard** for analyzing auto insurance policies and claim amounts. It summarizes key policy metrics and compares claim amounts across age groups, car years, education levels, coverage zones, car companies, and car usage.

## 🧩 Business Objective

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of insurance policies and claim amounts to help users explore how claims are distributed across customer and vehicle categories.

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators

- **Number of Policies:** 37,542
- **Total Claim Amount:** $187.8M
- **Average Claim Amount:** $5.00K
- **No-Claim Policy %:** 72.46%
- **Policies by Gender:** Male — 18,736; Female — 18,806

## 🎛️ Dashboard Filters

- **Coverage Zone**
- **Car Use**

These filters allow users to explore the dashboard for selected coverage zones and car-use categories.

## 📊 Dashboard Visuals

| Visual | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Column Chart — Claim Amount by Age Group | Compares total claim amounts across the displayed age groups |
| Line Chart — Claim Amount by Car Year | Shows how total claim amount varies by car year |
| Column Chart — Claim Amount by Education | Compares claim amounts across education levels |
| Donut Chart — Claim Amount by Coverage Zone | Shows the distribution of claim amount across coverage zones |
| Horizontal Bar Chart — Claim Amount by Car Company | Compares claim amounts for the displayed car companies |
| Pie Chart — Claim Amount by Car Use | Shows the claim-amount split by car-use category |
| Gender Summary | Displays the number of male and female policyholders |

## 🔎 Highlights Visible in the Dashboard

- The dashboard reports **37,542 policies** and a **total claim amount of $187.8M**.
- The **average claim amount** is shown as **$5.00K**, and the **no-claim policy percentage** is **72.46%**.
- In the education chart, **Bachelors** has the largest displayed claim amount at **$94M**, followed by **High School** at **$52M**.
- In the car-company chart, **Ford** has the largest displayed claim amount at **$16.6M**, followed by **Chevrolet** at **$14.8M**.
- The car-use pie chart displays an **80% / 20%** split in claim amount.
- The male and female policy counts are close: **18,736 male** and **18,806 female**.

*These highlights describe the values shown in the dashboard; they do not establish causes or risk levels.*

## 🛠️ Tools

- **Power BI** — dashboard and data visualization

## 📌 Conclusion

The Auto Insurance Claims Analysis Dashboard brings key policy metrics and claim-amount comparisons into one view. Its KPIs, category-based visuals, and filters make it easier to explore the displayed insurance data by age group, car year, education, coverage zone, car company, and car use.
